# Lore and source guidelines

Lore.io separates three kinds of content:

- `documented`: information presented as sourced real-world or officially
  documented material.
- `fictional`: content belonging to an established fictional universe.
- `fan-made`: community theories, reinterpretations, and original additions.

Each entry should state its classification and avoid presenting fan-made
claims as fact. A myth can combine classifications, but the UI should make the
distinction visible before a player treats an entry as evidence.

Content contributors should prefer a satisfying, honest explanation over
artificial mystery. If a conclusion is intentionally unresolved, document what
the player can establish and why the remaining uncertainty is part of the
experience.
